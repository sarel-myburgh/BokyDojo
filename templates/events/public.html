But "no-referrer" also makes the browser send "Origin: null" on this page's own form post, and Django's CSRF check rejects a null origin: every reply came back 403 Forbidden. curl never sends an Origin header, so the tests and the manual walk-through both missed it. "same-origin" sends the full referrer only to us and nothing at all to another site, which protects the token and leaves the form working.
